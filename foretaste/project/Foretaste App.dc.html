Why the data holds up

Incentives that don't bend the answer.

Panelists are paid for completed, quality-reviewed responses — never for positive ones.

Businesses cannot reject a response for being critical; Foretaste adjudicates quality, not sentiment.

Every response passes attention, timing and duplication checks.

Feedback is private research and is never published as a public review.
