What HiTerra Is

A working tool for the people who actually farm the land

Growers and field agronomists capture what happens in the field — on a phone, offline, in their own language.

HiTerra turns that into agronomic recommendations, task plans people can follow, and records that hold up when someone audits them.

How It Works

From the field to the balance sheet

01 HiTerra The mobile app. Field data capture, built for low bandwidth.
02 TerraBrain AI agronomic recommendations, tied to the crop and the cycle.
03 TerraTask Tasks and crop cycles — assigned, done, logged.
04 TerraCarbon Carbon measurement and reporting from the same field records.
05 HiTerra Dashboard The enterprise view across every field and every grower.
